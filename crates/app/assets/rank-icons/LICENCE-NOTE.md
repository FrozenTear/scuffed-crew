## What the grant covers here, and what it does not

- The site relies on the grant to **display** the emblems on a noncommercial
  community site, with Blizzard's notice kept: every page that shows an
  emblem also shows, once, in small muted text: "Overwatch rank emblems ©
  Blizzard Entertainment, Inc. Overwatch and Blizzard are trademarks of
  Blizzard Entertainment, Inc." (`credit()` in
  `crates/app/src/pages/stats/rank.rs`).
- **Keeping the files in this public repository is not covered by the
  grant.** A public git repository hands a copy to everyone who clones it,
  which goes past "one copy" and "non-transferable". This is FrozenTear's
  accepted decision, not something the licence grants.
- **The dx repack is not covered by the grant.** `dx build --release`
  repacks every PNG it bundles losslessly, these files included: the shipped
  files keep the decoded pixels but not the bytes, which is a modification.
  This is also FrozenTear's accepted decision, not something the licence
  grants. The files in the repository stay as downloaded.
- **On any request from Blizzard, FrozenTear will remove the files** from the
  repository and the site. If the grant is revoked or Blizzard asks, the
  emblems and the credit line go together, and the rank chart falls back to
  the tier names it always shows in words.

## Do not edit these files

Do not edit, recompress, resize or replace these files. The emblems are only
scaled in CSS, never recoloured, cropped or edited. The test
`emblem_files_are_unchanged` in `crates/app/src/pages/stats/rank.rs` pins
each file's byte length and FNV-1a hash and fails on any change.

## Why they are in git

The root `.gitignore` ignores `*.png` (screenshots and captures); these files
have their own `!crates/app/assets/rank-icons/*.png` exception, like the
favicons. They must be in the repository because `asset!()` fails the build
on a checkout without them. They are the one approved exception to the rule
that game art is never committed (see `docs/tracker-packs.md` and
`docs/deploy.md`).
